Critical Findings
🔴 Biggest Drop-off
91.4%
Of new visitors never reach /signup. Homepage → Signup is the weakest step. Only 103 of 1,194 new users clicked through. This is the #1 thing to fix with ad landing pages.
🔴 Signup Bounce (New Users)
48.3%
New users bounce from /signup at 48.3% — far worse than the 21% blended average we previously reported. Returning users bounce 0%. The UX redesign is critical.
🟡 Chat → Billing Gap
77%
77% of new users who use /chat do not visit /billing. Only 17 of 74 chat users showed upgrade intent. Need in-app upgrade prompts after first chat session.
🟢 Product Retention
76.5%
Returning users have 76.5% engagement rate vs 39.5% for new. 676 returning /chat sessions proves the product creates habits. Retention is strong — acquisition is the gap.
🟢 Form Completion
95.1%
Once new users start the signup form, 95.1% complete it. The problem is getting them to /signup in the first place — not the form itself.
🟡 Organic Social Quality
70.4%
Organic Social (LinkedIn/Instagram) brings 74 new users with 70.4% engagement — the highest quality channel after Organic Search. Paid social will likely perform even better with targeting.
